About the founder

Ruhan is the solo founder of LitigationOS, developing the product around the everyday coordination needs of Indian litigation chambers.

From the public website to the evolving chamber workspace, the project is being built with a focus on making matter information easier to follow and act on.

Why LitigationOS

A legal matter involves more than its next hearing. It also involves responsibilities, expenses, internal notes and communication with the client.

The idea behind LitigationOS is to bring those connected activities into a clearer shared view, so the team can understand what happened and what needs to happen next.

Current focus

The current release focuses on shared matter records, team tasks, deadlines, client costs and owner-reviewed hearing updates.

LitigationOS is an early release. Explore the product status to distinguish available features from previews and future plans.

A longer-term vision

The aim is a matter-centred operating system that helps chambers coordinate their work and communicate more clearly with clients.

Future development is guided by practical chamber workflows. Planned capabilities are described separately from what is available today.
