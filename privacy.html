LitigationOS / Privacy & data handling

Privacy & data handling

A plain-language notice describing the current release. Last updated 2 November 2026. For questions or a data request, contact Ruhan@litigationos.in.

Who operates this product

LitigationOS is a founder-led project operated by Ruhan. It provides a website, a demonstration workspace and a signed-in Shared Chamber area. This notice describes implemented behaviour; it is not a claim of independent security certification.

Website and investor inquiries

The pilot and investor forms prepare a draft in your email app. The page does not submit or save those fields. You choose whether to send the email. If you send it, the founder and the email services involved process your name, email, organisation and message so the founder can respond. Do not include confidential case information in an inquiry.

Sign-in and Shared Chamber

Firebase handles account sign-in. The app uses your account ID, email and profile information for sign-in and display. The Shared Chamber service verifies your Firebase token and verified email before processing a request.

Supabase stores chamber names, team membership and roles, invitation records, matters, tasks, deadlines, expenses, hearing outcomes, internal notes, client drafts, approval metadata and change history. Change history can include earlier versions of record content. Invitation codes are stored as hashes and are tied to an email, expire after seven days and work once.

Owners control invitations and access. Internal members can edit records. Viewers can read and export chamber records, including internal notes. These are staff roles; the Client demo does not grant access to shared chamber data. The founder administers the infrastructure. Do not treat a UI role as protection against authorised infrastructure administration.

Browser-local demo information and files

Some older demo tasks and spending entries use localStorage on the device. Clearing browser storage removes those local entries; doing so does not delete shared database records. The demo document picker lists selected files in the browser session. It does not upload those files to shared storage.

Shared Chamber records are fetched from the database rather than cached in localStorage. Authentication services may store session information in the browser. Exporting a backup or approved update creates a file on your device, which you are responsible for handling appropriately.

AI requests

The website assistant sends your product question and up to six preceding conversation messages to the backend and then to OpenRouter and a selected model provider. Its conversation is held in the current page session. The app’s AI Associate sends submitted questions to its AI service as well. These AI requests are distinct from ordinary Shared Chamber saves; recording a hearing does not automatically send its notes to AI.

Provider processing and retention depend on the selected service and its policies. LitigationOS does not promise zero retention or that all model providers exclude training. Avoid personal, privileged or confidential material in AI questions. AI responses can be inaccurate and should be reviewed.

Hosting, providers and locations

The website and app use Vercel hosting, Firebase authentication, Supabase database/Edge Functions and OpenRouter for AI routing. Providers may process operational logs such as request metadata, IP addresses and errors. This release does not add advertising analytics or tracking pixels.

The current Supabase project is in the Seoul region. Other services may process information in other locations. We do not claim India-only data residency.

Provider information: Firebase privacy, Supabase privacy, Vercel privacy, OpenRouter privacy.

Retention, access and deletion

No automatic deletion schedule is configured for chamber records or change history in this release. Invitation expiry does not automatically delete its record. The downloaded backup feature is manual and does not create an automatic backup or restore service. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

To request access, correction, deletion or help with a lost account, email the founder. We may need to verify your identity and authority over the chamber before changing records. Tell us the relevant chamber and request, without sending identification documents or confidential case files unless a suitable process has been agreed.

Before a confidential pilot

Discuss the intended data, access requirements, provider terms, retention and backup arrangements with the founder before using confidential client material. Keep copies of important records and verify dates against the court record.
