Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about today’s release.

Before you start

Clear answers to important questions.

Is LitigationOS a demo or a shared workspace?

Both are available. The original Professional and Client views contain example data and browser-local features. Shared Chamber is a separate signed-in area for shared matters, tasks, deadlines, expenses and hearing records.

Can my clients log into the real client portal?

The Client view is currently a preview. Real client accounts and secure client file sharing are still planned. Owner-approved hearing summaries can be copied or downloaded for manual sharing.

Do document uploads go to shared storage?

No. The current document picker lists files locally in the demo. It does not upload them to a shared document room.

Does the app fetch court dates or send reminders automatically?

Not yet. Court-wide synchronisation and automated email or mobile reminders remain planned. Dates in Shared Chamber are entered by the chamber and should be checked against the court record.

How does the app use AI?

The website assistant answers product questions. The app also has an AI Associate interface. AI requests use OpenRouter and model providers. Avoid confidential information and review responses before relying on them. See privacy and data handling.

What does a pilot cost?

No public paid plan has been published. Contact Ruhan to discuss your chamber’s needs, onboarding and any proposed pilot terms before deciding to proceed.
